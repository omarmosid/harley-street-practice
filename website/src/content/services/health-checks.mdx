## Why we run health checks the way we do

A health check is only as useful as the conversation that follows it. Many private medicals deliver a stack of numbers without context; we begin with the conversation and let the tests serve it. Your doctor takes a careful history first, performs the examination personally, and discusses every result with you in plain English before sending the written report.

That structure is consistent across every check we offer — from the fifteen-minute Basic screen to the multi-hour Executive medical. The difference between tiers is breadth of investigation, not quality of attention.

## Choosing the right check

If this is your first private medical and you are otherwise well, the **Premium Health Check** or the **Essential Health Screen** offers a thorough annual baseline. If you are over forty, an annual **Well Man** or **Well Woman** check provides full age-appropriate screening with a follow-up included. For comprehensive cancer screening with imaging, the **Executive** package is the most complete offering we have.

If you are unsure, call us. We will not upsell — most patients fit comfortably in the £255–£690 range, and we would rather you book a smaller check and add tests on the day than commit to one too large.

## What every check includes

Regardless of tier, every health check includes:

- An unhurried consultation with one of our experienced physicians
- A full physical examination conducted by your doctor (not delegated)
- A written, personalised report with a clear action plan
- A follow-up appointment to discuss results and next steps

Specific tests vary by tier — the [comparison above](#choose-your-check) lists exactly what is included in each.
