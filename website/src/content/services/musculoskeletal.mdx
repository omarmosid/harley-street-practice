## How musculoskeletal care fits into the practice

Pain in the back, neck, shoulders, hips, or knees is one of the most common reasons people see a doctor — and one of the slowest things to resolve through fragmented care. We brought musculoskeletal treatment in-house so that diagnosis, manual treatment, imaging, and any onward referrals can happen under one roof and in the same record.

In practice this means a patient with persistent lower-back pain might see the GP for a clinical history and bloods to rule out organic cause, have an MRI arranged through our radiology partners, and begin chiropractic treatment with Justin — all coordinated, and all on Harley Street.

## What we treat

Our chiropractor sees the full range of mechanical and soft-tissue conditions:

- Lower-back pain, including muscle strain, sciatica, and spinal-alignment issues
- Neck pain, tension, and posture-related problems
- Shoulder injuries — rotator cuff, impingement, frozen shoulder
- Hip and knee pain, including arthritis-related and sports-related cases
- Repetitive strain injuries affecting elbows, wrists, and ankles
- Pregnancy-related musculoskeletal pain
- Post-injury rehabilitation alongside specialist orthopaedic care

If your case requires advanced imaging or specialist orthopaedic input, we can arrange both within our trusted Harley Street network.
