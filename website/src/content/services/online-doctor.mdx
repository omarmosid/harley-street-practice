## Why we offer virtual consultations

Not every concern requires you to come to the clinic, and many patients value the flexibility of being able to see their doctor from home, an office, or while travelling. Online consultations let us provide the same thirty-minute, doctor-led care without the commute — useful for follow-ups, prescription reviews, mental-health support, and the routine medical questions that come up between in-person visits.

For patients living outside London, or travelling for work, the online service offers continuity with the same doctor and the same practice. Your record stays in one place, and any tests or imaging we arrange feed back into it.

## What an online consultation can do

A virtual appointment can:

- Address general health concerns and provide advice
- Manage chronic and ongoing conditions
- Issue prescriptions (electronically, to your chosen pharmacy)
- Arrange blood tests, urine analysis, and imaging at our clinic or partner facilities
- Refer to specialists within our trusted Harley Street network
- Provide mental-health support alongside our therapy service
- Handle urgent same-day questions where physical examination is not essential

Where a hands-on examination is essential — and the doctor will tell you when it is — we will switch you to an in-person appointment, often the same day.

## Privacy and security

Consultations run on encrypted video. Your record is held under the same standards as our in-person care, and is accessible to the clinical team caring for you and to no one else. We do not record consultations.
