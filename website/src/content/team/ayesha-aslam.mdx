Ayesha Aslam is a Systemic Therapist with over 20 years of experience, specialising in supporting families, couples, and individuals. As a Clinical Supervisor, Keynote speaker, Consultant, Trainer, and Founder of her own counselling service, she tackles a wide range of issues, from everyday challenges to intricate psychological concerns.

On a personal level, Ayesha embraces challenges and new experiences with enthusiasm. She enjoys travelling to new destinations, experimenting with diverse cuisines, learning street fighting techniques, dancing, and singing her favourite tunes at karaoke.
