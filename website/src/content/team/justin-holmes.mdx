Justin is a highly experienced McTimoney Chiropractor with decades of expertise in spinal health, posture and ergonomics. Recently he has offered specialised care for women in postpartum recovery. A sought-after public speaker on these topics, Justin's background includes work as a Physical Therapist and Personal Trainer for World Champion Ballroom and Latin dancers in both the USA and Hong Kong. His career also spans paediatric occupational therapy, where he specialised in the rehabilitation of neuromuscular disorders.

With extensive healthcare experience in the UK and internationally, Justin is passionate about helping others achieve their health goals. Outside of work, he enjoys an active lifestyle, playing tennis, practising martial arts, and spending quality time with his young daughter and their beloved dog.
