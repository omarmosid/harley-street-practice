Dr Mariyah Iqbal is an experienced General Practitioner with over 10 years of experience graduating from The University of Leeds in 2011.

Dr Iqbal is passionate about medical education and continues to teach at 3 of the top Universities in London; Imperial College London, Queen Mary's University London and at University College London. Having obtained a diploma from The Royal College of Obstetrics and Gynaecology, she specialises in Women's Health.

Dr Iqbal enjoys reading and travelling.
