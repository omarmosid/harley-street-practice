Dr Michelle McCreesh is a kind and compassionate doctor with 20 years experience as a General Practioner. She has a wealth of experience across the full spectrum of General Practice and makes sure that her patients have access to the very best resources in health care.

She is a member of the Royal College of General Practioner's (RCGP) having passed the exams with Merit. She has many different interests within medicine and is particularly passionate about Lifestyle Medicine and Wellness.

In her personal life her two young children keep her busy as they approach their teenage years. She enjoys going for long walks with her scruffy doodle at the weekend, and she enjoys cooking healthy food for her family and keeping active with indoor cycling, Pilates and yoga.
