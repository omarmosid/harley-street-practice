Dr Ahmed Bachelani, a highly esteemed physician on Harley Street, graduated from The Royal Free Hospital School of Medicine in 1997. During his medical training, he also achieved a BSc in Immunopathology. Following his training, he pursued specialist training as a hospital physician, becoming a Member of the Royal College of Physicians. Subsequently, he chose to focus on General Practice, earning membership in the Royal College of General Practitioners.

Dr Bachelani is deeply dedicated to General Practice and takes pride in helping patients enhance their health and overall well-being. In recognition of his exceptional care, he received an award in 2019.

With over 25 years of medical expertise, Dr Bachelani brings a wealth of experience to benefit his patients. He places great emphasis on attentive listening, addressing patient concerns, and prioritising their health needs in a professional, friendly, and engaging manner.

Outside of medicine, he enjoys pursuits such as landscape gardening, painting, and writing children's books. Dr Bachelani specialises in occupational health and managing complex chronic medical conditions.
