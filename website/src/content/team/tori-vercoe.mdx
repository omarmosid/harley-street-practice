Tori Is the main Clinic Receptionist, she takes care of coordinating appointments, answering patients queries and handling daily administrative tasks.

Tori is the welcoming face of London Harley street Practice. With a strong focus on organisation and customer care she strives to create a warm and supportive clinic environment where patients feel informed and at ease.

Outside of work, she enjoys exploring other places and cultures around the world, discovering different cuisines at new food spots or trying recipes at home. She keeps active with swimming, gym sessions and country walks, including discovering the next best coffee shop in town. Tori loves spending quality time with her friends and family.
