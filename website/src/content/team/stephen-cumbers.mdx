Dr Cumbers graduated from University College London Medical School in 2011. He is a Member of the Royal College of General Practitioners. He has interests in paediatrics and andrology.

His warm and approachable demeanour creates a comfortable clinical environment. He readily engenders trust in the shy or anxious of all ages. He believes that kindness is the mainspring of medicine and the basis of all sound health-care advice.

Dr Cumbers is a Chartered Scientist (CSci) with a Master's degree in Analytical Chemistry and Fellowship of the Institute of Biomedical Science. He holds Life Membership of the Royal Society of Chemistry and various other learned societies. His experience includes medical student teaching, and he was sometime visiting lecturer in quantitative finance at UEL.

In his free time, he pursues a lifelong fascination for mathematics and physics. He continues to be a Member of the London Mathematical Society, to which he was admitted in 1986. He often attends conferences, and has organized symposia on computing.
