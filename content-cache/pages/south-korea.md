### South Korean Visa Medical

When travelling to South Korea, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. South Korea’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, stool tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

South Korea is a technologically advanced country with excellent healthcare. English is widely spoken in major cities. Ensure you have travel insurance and check for any vaccination requirements before your visit.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Gyeongbokgung** Palace in Seoul, a historical site from the Joseon Dynasty. Another popular destination is **Namsan Seoul Tower**, offering panoramic views of the city.
