### Ghana Immigration Medical

When travelling to Ghana, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Ghana’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, stool tests, and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Ghana is known for its friendly people and rich history. Malaria (Plasmodium falciparum)  is prevalent, so take antimalarial precautions. Ensure your routine vaccinations are up to date. 

The most iconic place to visit is **Cape Coast Castle**, a significant historical site from the horrific transatlantic slave trade era. Another must-see is **Kakum National Park**, with its canopy walkway.

### Tests Included

-   Physical examination
    
-   Blood tests for the following:  
    Full Blood Count, HIV, Syphilis, Hepatitis B, Blood group
    
-   Stool Sample (Ova, Cysts & Parasites)
    
-   Urinalysis
    
-   Chest x-ray to exclude TB infection
