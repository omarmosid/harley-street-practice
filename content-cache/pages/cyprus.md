### Cyprus Visa Medical

When travelling to Cyprus, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Cyprus’ visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Cyprus offers beautiful beaches and a rich cultural history. Ensure you have valid travel insurance and stay hydrated, as Cyprus can be very hot, especially in summer. English is widely spoken, and the country is generally safe.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Aphrodite’s Rock**, a legendary site linked to the goddess of love. Another key site is the **Tombs of the Kings** in Paphos.
