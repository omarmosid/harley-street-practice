### Taiwan Immigration Medical

When travelling to Taiwan, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Taiwan’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Taiwan is known for its friendly people and modern cities. Mandarin is the official language, but English is spoken in tourist areas. Please make sure you have comprehensive travel insurance.

The most iconic place to visit is **Taipei 101**, one of the world’s tallest buildings. Another popular destination is **Taroko Gorge**, known for its scenic marble cliffs.
