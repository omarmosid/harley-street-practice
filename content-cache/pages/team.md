## Dr Ahmed Bachelani

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6485f94066549020ed1a7a14/08dfbc69-ab2e-49cd-8840-42af174d3353/Dr+B+LHSP+v1+%281%29.png)

#### MBBS MRCP MRCGP DOccMed BSc

Dr Ahmed Bachelani, a highly esteemed physician on Harley Street, graduated from The Royal Free Hospital School of Medicine in 1997. During his medical training, he also achieved a BSc in Immunopathology. Following his training, he pursued specialist training as a hospital physician, becoming a Member of the Royal College of Physicians. Subsequently, he chose to focus on General Practice, earning membership in the Royal College of General Practitioners.   

Dr Bachelani is deeply dedicated to General Practice and takes pride in helping patients enhance their health and overall well-being. In recognition of his exceptional care, he received an award in 2019.   

With over 25 years of medical expertise, Dr Bachelani brings a wealth of experience to benefit his patients. He places great emphasis on attentive listening, addressing patient concerns, and prioritising their health needs in a professional, friendly, and engaging manner.  

Outside of medicine, he enjoys pursuits such as landscape gardening, painting, and writing children's books. Dr Bachelani specialises in occupational health and managing complex chronic medical conditions.

## Dr Stephen Cumbers

#### MBBS MSc MRCGP CSci MRSC FIBMS  
**General Practitioner**

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6485f94066549020ed1a7a14/b6552b10-64d1-406a-a56e-80abfac19129/updated+Dr+Cumbers+v2.jpg)

Dr Cumbers graduated from University College London Medical School in 2011. He is a Member of the Royal College of General Practitioners. He has interests in paediatrics and andrology.  
His warm and approachable demeanour creates a comfortable clinical environment. He readily engenders trust in the shy or anxious of all ages. He believes that kindness is the mainspring of medicine and the basis of all sound health-care advice.  
  
Dr Cumbers is a Chartered Scientist (CSci) with a Master's degree in Analytical Chemistry and Fellowship of the Institute of Biomedical  
Science. He holds Life Membership of the Royal Society of Chemistry and various other learned societies. His experience includes medical student teaching, and he was sometime visiting lecturer in quantitative finance at UEL.  
In his free time, he pursues a lifelong fascination for mathematics and physics. He continues to be a Member of the London Mathematical Society, to which he was admitted in 1986. He often attends conferences, and has organized symposia on computing.

## Dr Claudia Tailor

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6485f94066549020ed1a7a14/94d1fdf3-bee8-453e-8751-5db5587a5e8e/claudia+final+2025.png)

#### MBBS MRCGP  
**General Practitioner**

Dr Claudia Tailor is an experienced General Practitioner who has worked as an NHS doctor for over fifteen years. She completed her medical degree at King’s College London in 2010 and was awarded MRCGP in 2019.

Dr Tailor served as the Clinical Lead for a PCN-wide Paediatric Integrated Hub, a role focused on successfully bridging the critical care gap between primary and secondary paediatric services. Furthermore, she contributed to regional system change through leadership involvement in updating the North Central London Paediatric Asthma Guidelines. She is passionate about preventative care, and is completing her Postgraduate Diploma in Lifestyle Medicine, alongside her Management of Menopause Certificate with the British Menopause Society. In future, she plans to do her Level 7 Diploma in Aesthetic Medicine.

Dr Tailor is fluent in English and Portuguese, and speaks Spanish to a high standard. In her spare time, she enjoys days out with her young family.
