### British Virgin Islands Immigration Medical

When travelling to the British Virgin Islands, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This exam ensures that travellers are fit to enter the country and free from communicable diseases like tuberculosis. The visa medical process in the British Virgin Islands typically includes a straightforward physical examination, blood tests, urine tests, and a chest X-ray to screen for TB.

#### Travelling Information

The British Virgin Islands offer serene beaches and excellent sailing opportunities. Ensure you have comprehensive travel insurance, as healthcare services can be expensive. The climate is warm and tropical, so bring sunscreen and stay hydrated. English is the official language, making communication easy.

The most iconic place to visit is **The Baths on Virgin Gorda**, known for its unique rock formations and crystal-clear waters.
