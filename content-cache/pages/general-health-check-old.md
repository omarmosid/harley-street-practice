# Health Checks

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6485f94066549020ed1a7a14/2407e638-a05f-486e-9ff4-736d2aa1ecb7/iStock-1226087847.jpg)

#### At **London Harley Street Practice**, we offer a **variety** of health screening packages, tailored to how thoroughly you wish to assess your health. From basic annual health checks to more in-depth evaluations, our packages are designed to meet your **individual needs** and goals. 

#### Each package provides a comprehensive view of your current health, helping to address any concerns, motivate improvements, and detect early signs of potential issues. 

#### Our approach focuses on early detection, offering personalised advice, treatment, or referral if necessary. By emphasising preventative care, we empower you to take control of your health, ensuring peace of mind and a proactive approach to long-term wellness. **Your health is our priority**.

## How To Book Your Appointment

### Step One

Choose your required type of health check by first clicking on the box “30 minutes GP Consultation” and then scrolling through the options until you get to your required health check appointment. Please note that “Basic” and “Advanced” health checks can only be booked by calling the clinic.

### Step Two

Select a date and time that works for you. 

### Step Three

Fill in the form below with your full details. Full payment is required for each health check requested

For the “Plus” and “Executive” types as mentioned the imaging is not guaranteed to be on the same day as booked online. We would advise you to call us to arrange this for you unless you are happy to have this done on a different day. Please be aware that if you have any contraindications to an MRI scan then it will not be possible to undergo this package. Please call us to discuss your options.
