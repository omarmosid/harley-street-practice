### **What Happens During Your Consultation?**

Your **private GP consultation** typically lasts 30 minutes, allowing time for a thorough and unhurried assessment.

During your appointment, your doctor will:

-   Discuss your symptoms and medical history
    
-   Perform a relevant examination
    
-   Arrange investigations if required (e.g. blood tests, imaging)
    
-   Provide a clear diagnosis & treatment plan
    
-   Offer referrals to specialists where appropriate
    

Our focus is on delivering **clear, actionable medical advice**.
