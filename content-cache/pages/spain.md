### Spain Visa Medical

Travellers to Spain with certain visa requirements may need to undergo a visa medical examination. This medical assessment ensures that visitors are fit for entry and do not carry communicable diseases such as tuberculosis. Spain’s visa medical typically includes a physical examination, blood tests, urine tests, and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Spain is a popular destination known for its rich culture, history, and vibrant cities. It has good healthcare, but travel insurance is recommended. Spanish is the official language, though English is spoken in tourist areas.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Alhambra** in Granada, a stunning palace built during the Muslim Andalucian history of Spain. Another must-see is the **Sagrada Familia** in Barcelona, an architectural masterpiece.
