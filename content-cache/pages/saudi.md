### Saudi Arabia Visa Medical

When travelling to Saudi Arabia, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Saudi Arabia’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Like every country, Saudi Arabia has certain cultural norms, so it is important to follow local customs and dress conservatively. Ensure you have appropriate travel insurance and check if any vaccinations are required. Arabic is the official language, but English is spoken in many areas. See our travel blog in [**Insights**](</insights?tag=saudi arabia>) to learn more about the culture of Saudi Arabia and the peninsula in general.

### How to book your appointment

#### **Step One**

Choose “Medical for Visa” by first clicking on the box “GP Consultation: (30 min Appointment)” and then scrolling through the options until you get to “Medical for Visa”

#### **Step Two**

Select a date and time that works for you. If your Visa medical requires a chest x-ray, please do not book an appointment after 3.30pm. 

#### **Step Three**

Fill in the form below with your full details. A standard deposit for a Visa Medical is set at £170. 

When you are at the clinic you will be expected to pay the remaining amount depending on which Visa Medical you undergo. 

By giving us a call or booking online you are able to see an experienced doctor at your convenience without having to join any queues found at other walk-in clinics.
