### Brunei Visa Medical

When travelling to Brunei, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Brunei’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, stool tests, and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Brunei, located on Borneo Island in Southeast Asia, is a small yet wealthy nation known for its rich Islamic culture and lush rainforests. The official language is Malay, specifically the Brunei Malay dialect, which is widely spoken in daily life. However, most Bruneians are bilingual or multilingual, making communication accessible for English speaking visitors. 

The capital of Brunei, Bandar Seri Begawan, is home to the stunning Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque, which is considered a masterpiece of Islamic architecture. For a glimpse of traditional Bruneian life, explore Ulu Temburong National Park, a pristine rainforest with canopy walks, and Kampong Ayer, the world’s largest water village. Brunei’s peaceful vibe, vibrant markets, and hospitable locals make it a unique travel destination. 

Brunei is generally considered malaria-free, making it a safer destination compared to some other tropical regions. However, dengue fever, transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes, is a potential risk, especially during the rainy season. Travelers should take precautions such as using insect repellent, wearing long sleeves and trousers, and staying in accommodations with proper mosquito protection.
