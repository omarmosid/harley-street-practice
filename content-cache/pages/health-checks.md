# Stress Health Check

### £775

* * *

A new 2 part health check, the first on assessing and testing for organic causes of stress/anxiety followed by professional psychological assessment. 

#### 1st Part

Consultation with an experienced Physician to assess for underlying organic causes of stress.

-   Duration 45 minutes. 
    
-   Cardiovascular / Respiratory / Abdominal / Neurological Examinations.
    
-   Height, weight and Body Mass Index.
    
-   Blood tests (more than 20 specific lab readings): Haematology profile (including white cell count and ESR), Full Biochemistry profile relating to liver and kidney Iron status, uric acid, Bone profile (incl. Magnesium levels) and glucose and total cholesterol.
    
-   Vitamin D levels.
    
-   Inflammatory markers: ESR & CRP.
    
-   Cortisol blood level.
    
-   Thyroid hormone profile test.
    
-   Urine analysis to check for blood, white cells, protein, glucose and ketones.
    
-   6 lead ECG for Heart Rate and Rhythm.
    

#### 2nd Part

Consultation with a qualified Psychotherapist to assess stress and mental health (video online). Including some treatment.

-   Duration 60 minutes.
    
-   Personalised reports, highlighting problems, health advice and an action plan.
    

The 1st Part (in clinic) can be booked via the website's online booking page "Heath Check: Stress (Medical)". Your 2nd Part with the Psychologist will be booked after this by the clinic to suit your schedule (or you can call/email us to book that for you).

Select a date and time that works for you.\*\*

Fill in the form below with your full details.

By giving us a call or booking online you are able to book your health check at your convenience with an experienced Harley Street doctor.
