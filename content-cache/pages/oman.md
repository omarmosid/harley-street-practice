### Oman Visa Medical

When travelling to Oman, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Oman’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, stool tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Oman is known for its stunning natural beauty and rich history. The climate is very hot, so travellers should stay hydrated. Arabic is the official language, though English is commonly spoken in tourist areas. Oman is a safe and welcoming country.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque** in Muscat, known for its stunning architecture. **Wadi Shab** offers a spectacular outdoor adventure with its turquoise pools and caves.
