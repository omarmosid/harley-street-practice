### Bermuda Immigration Medical

When travelling to Bermuda, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Bermuda’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Bermuda is known for its pink-sand beaches and past colonial history. Ensure your travel insurance is up to date and be prepared for warm, humid weather. 

The most iconic place to visit is **Horseshoe Bay Beach**, known for its pink sand. Another key attraction is **St. George’s**, a UNESCO World Heritage site showcasing Bermuda’s early colonial architecture.
