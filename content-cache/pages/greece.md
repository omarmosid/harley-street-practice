### Greek Visa Medical

A visa medical examination is required to travel to Greece under specific visa types. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy and do not carry communicable diseases like tuberculosis. The visa medical process in Greece generally involves a simple physical examination, blood tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Greece offers a rich history and stunning islands. Be mindful of the hot climate in summer and ensure you have travel insurance. English is widely spoken in tourist areas, but learning a few Greek phrases is appreciated.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Acropolis of Athens**, a symbol of ancient Greek civilization. Another must-see is the island of **Santorini** with its breathtaking views.
