### Bahrain Immigration Visa Medical

When travelling to Bahrain, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Bahrain’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, stool tests, a 12 lead ECG and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

**Travelling Information**

Bahrain is country with a rich history, offering a blend of ancient and contemporary attractions. Its climate is hot, so stay hydrated and wear sunscreen. It is wise to respect local customs, especially during Ramadan or when visiting religious sites.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Bahrain Fort (Qal'at al-Bahrain)**, a UNESCO World Heritage site representing the ancient Dilmun civilization. Another popular destination is the **Al Fateh Grand Mosque**, one of the largest mosques in the region.
