### Kuwait Visa Medical

When travelling to Kuwait, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Kuwait’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, urine tests, and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Kuwait has a hot desert climate, so staying hydrated and wearing sunscreen is essential. Public health care is of a good standard, but comprehensive travel insurance is recommended. Arabic is the official language, though English is widely understood.

The most iconic place to visit is the **Kuwait Towers**, a symbol of the country’s modern architecture. **The Grand Mosque** is another popular attraction for visitors.
