### Barbados Visa Medical

When travelling to Barbados, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Barbados’ visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, stool tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Barbados is known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant culture, and laid-back atmosphere. It is essential to have valid travel insurance and ensure your vaccinations are up to date. The island’s tropical climate means it’s warm year-round, so bring light clothing and sunscreen. 

One of the most iconic places to visit is **Harrison’s Cave**, a stunning limestone cavern with streams, stalactites, and stalagmites. Additionally, **Bathsheba Beach** is famous for its natural beauty and powerful surf.
