### General Practitioner services

At **London Harley Street Practice**, we understand the importance of general practitioners as the first point of contact for health concerns. We provide comprehensive care, integrating physical, psychological, and social well-being, ensuring personalised, efficient, and holistic treatment for every patient. Should you require, we are happy to provide a [chaperone](/about-the-practice) for your appointment. [Learn more about our team](/our-team).

### Employment medicals

We can provide full employment and occupational health medicals - please call or email us to discuss details about what you require. [Learn more about our occupational health assessments.](/health-checks-1)

### Visa medicals

We provide comprehensive visa medicals to meet the requirements of various countries. Our expert team, of registered FCDO doctors, ensures efficient, thorough assessments for a smooth visa application process. [Find out more](/visa-and-immigration).

### Weight control and dietary advice

In order to reduce your weight in a sensible fashion, make an appointment to see our clinicians. Discover a healthy weight and happy dieting.

### Condition-specific assessments and management

We provide comprehensive condition-specific assessments and management for chronic health conditions, including hypertension, heart disease, diabetes mellitus, asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and arthritis. Each treatment plan is tailored to your needs, combining detailed diagnostics with personalised care to achieve the best outcomes. If required, we can facilitate referrals to leading specialists on Harley Street for advanced care. Our goal is to help you manage your condition effectively and enhance your quality of life.

### Allergy assessments

At London Harley Street Practice, we offer comprehensive allergy assessments for both adults and children, identifying triggers and managing symptoms effectively. Our expert team provides personalised care with advanced testing and treatment options. If necessary, we can refer you to leading allergy and dermatology specialists for further care.

### Fungal Nail Infections

We are able to treat and test for fungal infections. We can even arrange for laser therapy for resistant and persistent cases.

### STD Testing

Our rapid testing and treatment give you peace of mind all in a confidential and professional manner.

### General health checks

We offer extensive expertise in delivering a **variety** of health screens and checks tailored to individual needs and requirements. From comprehensive health assessments to focused screenings, our services are designed to provide personalised care and actionable insights. Whether for preventative measures or detailed diagnostics, we ensure a thorough and professional approach. [Find out more.](/general-health-check)

### Chiropractic services

Our in-house chiropractic services target pain relief and improved mobility for musculoskeletal issues. We offer personalised care for conditions like back and neck pain. [Learn more here.](/chiropractic)

### Counselling and Psychotherapy services

Our in-house therapy services are able to provide expert care and support for your mental health needs. We offer both online and face to face consultations. [Learn more here](/counselling-psychotherapy).

### Blood tests & Urine analysis

We can arrange for an array of varied blood tests from simple Haematology profiles to more specific blood tests screening for cancers and allergies.

### ECG

The Electrocardiogram is a test that measures the electrical activity of your heart. It is able to show any intrinsic problems and rhythm disorders.

### X-ray/MRI/CT/ Ultrasound Scans

We have a close relationship to our neighbouring diagnostic facilities so as to obtain prompt investigations by Radiologists of the highest calibre. We now provide onsite ultrasound scans which can be [booked today.](/ultrasound)

### Lung Function Tests

Lung function tests are conducted to identify any underlying breathing issues, such as asthma or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). These tests help assess lung health, ensuring early detection and effective management of respiratory conditions.

### Overseas patients and those here on holidays

We provide a friendly easily accessible service to all visitors to the UK. We understand the difficulties faced by any visitor to the UK in accessing excellent medical care. We are able to arrange home and hotel visits and provide private prescriptions and all other health services that are needed.
