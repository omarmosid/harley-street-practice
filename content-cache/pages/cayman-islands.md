### Cayman Islands Immigration Medical

When visiting the Cayman Islands, certain visa types require a visa medical examination to confirm travellers are in good health and free from communicable diseases like tuberculosis. The medical exam typically consists of a physical check, blood tests (often for HIV), and a chest X-ray to screen for TB.

#### Travelling Information

The Cayman Islands are known for their crystal-clear waters and marine life. The islands are generally safe, but it’s important to have valid travel insurance and be aware of local laws. The tap water is safe to drink, and English is the official language.

The most iconic place to visit is **Seven Mile Beach**, famous for its white sand and clear waters. Another must-see is **Stingray City**, where visitors can interact with friendly stingrays in shallow waters. Yes even we are surprised by this.
