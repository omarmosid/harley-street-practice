# Terms & Conditions

**London Harley Street Practice Membership Terms and Conditions**

**1\. Introduction**

Welcome to the London Harley Street Practice Membership programme. By enrolling in our membership tiers, you agree to the following Terms and Conditions. Please read them carefully before subscribing

**2\. Definitions**

-   **Member**: Any individual or family enrolled in a membership tier.
    
-   **Membership Benefits**: Services and privileges provided to members based on their membership tier.
    
-   **Child**: A child is aged from 0 to 16 years.  
    
-   **Adult**: Is either a parent or a legal guardian.
    
-   **Priority / Apex Family**: A membership package covering two adults and two children or 1 adult and 3 children.
    
-   **Clinic**: Refers to London Harley Street Practice (LHSP) based at 66 Harley Street, London, W1G 7HD.
    
-   **Start Date**: Your membership will begin once the first payment has been made and confirmed. 
    

**3\. Membership Tiers** We offer three membership tiers with associated benefits and fees:

**3.1 Starter Membership**

-   Fee: £120 annually (paid upfront).
    
-   Renewal: Automatically renews annually unless cancelled 30 days before the renewal date.
    

**3.2 Priority Membership**

-   Fee: £85 per month.
    
-   Minimum Term: 6 months. If cancelled before the end of the minimum term, the remaining months must still be paid in full.
    

**3.3 Apex Membership**

-   Fee: £115 per month.
    
-   Minimum Term: 12 months. If cancelled before the end of the minimum term, the remaining months must still be paid in full.
    

**4\. Family Packages** These are available for Priority and Apex Memberships. A family is defined as two adults and two children or 1 adult and 3 children.

**4.1 Family Pricing:**

-   Priority Family Membership: £250 per month.
    
-   Apex Family Membership: £320 per month.
    
-   Family bundles are subject to the same minimum term requirements as the individual memberships.

**9\. Cancellation Policy**

Right to Cancel

-   You have 14 days from the date of joining to exercise your right to cancel and receive a full refund.
    
-   To cancel, you must notify us within the 14-day period. The refund will be processed once we confirm your cancellation request.
    
-   After the Right to Cancel Period:
    
-   After the 14-day right to cancel period, you will be committed to the full term of the membership.
    
-   If you decide to cancel your membership after this period, no refund will be issued, and you will be responsible for paying the full remaining balance of the membership.
    

**Charges for Services Used During the Right to Cancel Period:**  
If you have used any services within the 14-day right to cancel period, those services will be charged at the normal rates, regardless of your cancellation request.  
**Renewal Cancellation**Starter Membership: Must be cancelled 30 days before the renewal date to avoid automatic renewal.  
Priority and Apex Memberships: Cannot be cancelled before fulfilling the minimum term. After the minimum term, a 30-day notice is required for cancellation.

**10\. Refund Policy**  
Refunds are not available for Starter Memberships or the remaining months of Priority or Apex Memberships if cancelled during the minimum term. Exceptions may apply for extenuating circumstances at the clinic’s discretion.
