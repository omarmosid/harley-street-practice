## Flexible Membership Plans

We offer three different tiers of annual membership, each allowing you to choose your preferred level of care and unlock personalised health care for your unique needs. As a member, you will enjoy priority access to appointments and discounted services to give you peace of mind. Our memberships are limited and thus are all subject to availability.  
*We also offer family packages to ensure the health of your entire family is covered. Say goodbye to long waits for appointments and rushed consultations that do not fully address your concerns. At London Harley Street Practice, we provide longer appointments, same-day bookings, shorter waiting times, and personalised care tailored to your needs. With access to rapid diagnostics and specialist referrals, we deliver a seamless, patient-focused service you can rely on.* This membership scheme is not linked to the NHS and does not replace it either.  
**Please see the application form at the end of this page to progress with your membership.**

## Membership Application

#### **Step 1**

Fill in the form on the right hand side to proceed with your membership application. Press submit. We will email you within 24 - 48h, please ensure that the details are correctly filled out. A membership form and a health questionnaire will be sent for you to fill out.

#### **Step 2**

Our team will then call you and answer any questions you have. You will have a free consultation with a doctor who will review your health questionnaire and address any health concerns.

#### **Step 3**

Our clinical team will review your application. Once approved you will receive a welcome letter, including payment details and instructions how to use your Membership tier benefits. You will be able to start using your Membership tier benefits following completion of the process.
