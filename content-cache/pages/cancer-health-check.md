# Cancer Health Checks - £1450

## Why should I consider a Cancer Screening Health Check?

Cancer rates are rising due to factors like lifestyle changes, diet, smoking, stress, and an ageing population. In the UK, 1 in 2 people born after 1960 are expected to be diagnosed with cancer at some point in their lives. Studies show that early detection of cancer leads to more effective treatment, lower treatment costs, and a higher likelihood of successful outcomes.

Each year, cancer accounts for roughly 4.1 million new cases and 1.8 million deaths across Europe, including the UK. With lifestyle changes and other risk factors, these figures are expected to grow.

## TruCheck™  
New cancer screening blood test

Our Cancer Screening Health Check includes a full examination and specifically uses a new and researched blood test to detect 70 cancers called TruCheck™. This allows the early detection of multiple cancers in asymptomatic individuals, including many cancers not typically covered by routine screenings. 

The TruCheck™ Early Cancer Screening blood test offers a comprehensive assessment for the presence of solid organ cancers, identifying the type and origin of any detected cancer. 

TruCheck™ is intended to be used in the **asymptomatic general population age 40 years and older, with no history of cancer**.

TruCheck™ tests are not recommended in the **following conditions**. *If you have any of these conditions then this test will not be available to you.*  

-   Patient with any cancer at present or in past (solid organ or hematolymphoid)
    
-   Patient in remission from cancer after treatment completion
    
-   Diagnosed benign condition with intent to rule out cancer
    
-   Any suspected cancer, clinically or radiologically
    
-   Blood transfusion in the last 72 hours prior to blood draw for TruCheck™
    
-   History of Lymphoma, Leukaemia, Polycythaemia, Plasmacytoma, Multiple myeloma, Essential thrombocythemia
    
-   History of Organ Transplant, Bone marrow transplants
    
-   Pregnancy
    
-   Immunocompromised conditions like HIV, Immunomodulator therapy,  Anti-immune therapy, Immunostimulant therapy
    

### **Can TruCheck™ be recommended for individuals under 40 years?**

TruCheck™, being a screening test, is advised for individuals aged 40 years and older, as ageing is an important risk factor for cancer. However, it can be offered to individuals aged 35-39 years if any of the following risk factors are applicable. 

-   A family history of cancer and/or known carrier status
    
-   Presence of risk-associated Hereditary germline mutation/s
    
-   A BMI above 30 kg/m2 (Obesity)
    
-   Type 2 diabetes
    
-   History of Infectious Diseases (such as HPV, Hepatitis B or Hepatitis C viruses)
    
-   Documented/ significant exposure to specific chemicals and carcinogens
    

This test can also be included at a discount in any other annual health check-up if requested.
