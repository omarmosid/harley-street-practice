### Indonesia Immigration Medical

When travelling to Indonesia, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Indonesia’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination and blood tests.

#### Travelling Information

Indonesia is known for its diverse islands, including Bali and Java. It is important to have up-to-date vaccinations and consider antimalarials depending on the region. Bahasa Indonesia is the official language, but English is spoken in tourist areas.

The most iconic place to visit is **Baiturrahman Grand Mosque**, which survived not only burning by Dutch colonists but the recent tsunami. Another top destination is **Bali**, famous for its beaches and temples.
