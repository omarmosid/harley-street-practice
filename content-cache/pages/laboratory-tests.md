### Whatever laboratory test you require please contact us to facilitate it.

Blood tests can be done at your appointment with no extra phlebotomy charge. The cost of the laboratory tests obviously varies and does not include the cost of the first consultation especially as the doctor will be able to further advise you on your options.

Usually, the results (blood mostly) will be available 4 hours afterwards. Please see individual tests (click here) to find out exactly the turnaround times. We ensure to send the results in a confidential link via email as soon as they are available. The doctor will also email any explanation necessary regarding these or even phone you if deemed clinically significant.

**Our tests can also be ordered without a GP Consultation, however we strongly advise you to see us first as we take the responsibility of ensuring the correct test is ordered and that a clinical assessment is made to best understand the results in context. For more information about what tests are available to ordered on line** [**tap here**](/onlinebloodtest)**.**
