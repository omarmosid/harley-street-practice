### Vietnam Immigration Medical

When travelling to Vietnam, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. Vietnam’s visa medical typically involves a straightforward process, including a physical examination, blood tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Vietnam is a beautiful country with diverse landscapes and a rich history. It is important to stay updated on vaccinations. Vietnamese is the official language, though English is commonly spoken in tourist areas.

The most iconic place to visit is **Ha Long Bay**, known for its emerald waters and limestone islands. **Hội An Ancient Town** is another popular destination, offering a glimpse of Vietnam’s historic architecture.
