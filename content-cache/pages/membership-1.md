## Membership Application

#### **Step 1**

Fill in the form on the right hand side to proceed with your membership application. Press submit. We will contact you within 24 - 48h.

#### **Step 2**

Our team will then call you and answer any questions you have. You will be sent a health questionnaire and a service agreement form to fill out for each person respectively to proceed.

#### **Step 3**

After you have returned this, our clinical team will review your application. Once approved you will receive a free welcome phone/video call with one of our doctors to discuss your health (optional).

#### **Step 4**

You will be able to start using your Membership tier benefits immediately.
