### Kazakhstan Visa Medical

When travelling to Kazakhstan, certain visa types require a visa medical examination. This medical ensures that travellers are healthy enough to enter the country and do not carry communicable diseases, such as tuberculosis. The certificate of good health medical involves a straightforward process and includes a physical examination, blood tests, urine tests and a chest X-ray to check for TB.

#### Travelling Information

Kazakhstan, the world’s largest landlocked country, offers a mix of natural beauty and modern attractions. Visit Almaty, the cultural hub, and explore the breathtaking Shymbulak Ski Resort or the serene Big Almaty Lake. In the capital Astana (Nur-Sultan), marvel at futuristic landmarks like the Bayterek Tower and the Khan Shatyr. Nature lovers can trek through Charyn Canyon, often compared to the Grand Canyon, or the vast steppes of Altai Mountains.

Whilst in Kazakhstan prepare for varied climates and in remote areas by carrying essentials. Respect cultural norms and stay aware of local road conditions in rural areas.

### Tests Included

-   Physical examination
    
-   Blood tests for Syphilis, HIV
    
-   Urinalysis including for STDs (Chlamydia and Gonorrhoea)
    
-   Urine Drug screen 
    
-   Chest X-ray to exclude TB infection
